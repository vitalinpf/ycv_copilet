Edit Copilot

Ask in plain words, review the plan, keep editing while it runs, then check or undo each change.

1
Open Copilot
Tap the Copilot button at the bottom-left of the timeline (it shows Edit by chatting for 3s on open). Hold it to speak.
2
Ask
Pick a suggestion, or type something like “smooth skin and make it 9:16” or “第2段慢一點”.
3
Review the plan
Nothing changes yet. Untick any step you don't want, then tap Run.
4
Keep editing
The sheet minimizes and the Copilot button becomes a progress pill. Tap it to see the task card and to-do list. Play the video or tap tools. Tap the pill or Stop to cancel.
5
Check the changes
Hold Compare on the preview. Undo a single change, or tap Adjust to fine-tune it in the tool.
6
Follow up
Try Make it stronger, Undo all, or the editor's Undo — a whole Copilot run is one undo step.
Copilot only uses tools marked Done for YCV in the competitor table: Trim, Crop, Speed, Reverse, Reorder, Split / Duplicate / Delete, Mute original audio, Transform, Retouch, Face Lift, Reshape, Makeup, Body Tuner, Hair, Looks, Adjust, Filters, Undo / Redo.
